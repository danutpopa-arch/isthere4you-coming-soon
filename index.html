What can IsThere4You mean in practice?

Sometimes all you need is a person who is already where you cannot be.

⌖ FORGOTTEN ITEM

Forgot something important?

Have you left town and realized you forgot your passport, keys, or another important item at the hotel? IsThere4You can connect you with someone who is there and may be able to help.

✓ CHECK BEFORE YOU GO

Want to see it before you travel?

Found an apartment, a car, or an item online hundreds of kilometres away? IsThere4You can connect you with someone nearby who can see the place or item before you make the trip.

◉ LOCAL INFORMATION

Need to know what is happening there?

Need to know what is happening right now in a particular place — whether a shop is open, how busy it is, or whether a product is still in stock? IsThere4You can connect you with someone who is there.

▣ VISUAL CHECK

Need someone to see it for you?

Need someone to photograph a place, a sign, a product, or a situation in another city? IsThere4You can connect you with someone who is there.

See more examples

Want to buy something in another city but do not know whether the product really exists or looks like the listing? IsThere4You can connect you with someone in the area who can check it for you.

Left luggage, a parcel, or a personal item somewhere you cannot easily return to? IsThere4You can help you find someone nearby.

Want to check an address, a location, or a situation before setting off? IsThere4You can connect you with someone who is already in the area.

Is something important to you hundreds or thousands of kilometres away? IsThere4You aims to turn distance into a connection with someone who is already there.

These examples illustrate only some of the situations in which IsThere4You may be useful; the possibilities are not limited to the cases shown here.
